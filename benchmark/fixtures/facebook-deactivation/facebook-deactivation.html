What happens to your data when you deactivate
Deactivation hides your profile, timeline, photos, and posts from other users. Logging back in within 30 days automatically restores the full account — there is no separate confirmation step, so an accidental login on any device (or a shared device used by family / coworkers) will silently restore your account. Messages you sent to friends will remain visible to those friends — they cannot be retracted. Your data continues to be retained on Facebook's servers for the duration of the deactivation. Third-party apps using Facebook Login that you previously connected to will continue to be able to look up your account via the API and may display you as available; you must revoke each integration separately. Permanent deletion (the other option above) initiates a 30-day grace period before data is removed; logging in during that window cancels the deletion request.
